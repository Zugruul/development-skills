## ADDED — §9.2 Mutation-boundary WIP admission

- WHEN a bundled board command directly or through queued replay requests entry into a counted status, the shared host-neutral mutation boundary SHALL enforce `methodology.maxInProgress` against a fresh complete snapshot of the selected board before the mutation or transition telemetry.
- WHERE `maxInProgress` is absent, admission SHALL use 1; WHERE present, it SHALL require a positive non-boolean integer. WHERE `serialDelivery` is absent, admission SHALL use false; WHERE present, it SHALL require a boolean. Unusable relevant policy or flow configuration SHALL refuse admission.
- The counted set SHALL include every active-board item in the work status (`statusFlow[1]`, or the sole status), plus review (`statusFlow[2]`, when present) WHERE `serialDelivery` is true, without filtering by spec or requiring a numbered issue to count occupancy. Unknown board IDs SHALL preserve the established first-board fallback. Requested status comparisons SHALL collapse whitespace and ignore case.
- WHEN the target already belongs to the counted set, admission SHALL permit its repeat/resume or move between counted statuses without another slot, subject to existing transition guards. WHEN a target outside the counted set requests entry, including direct entry into review in serial mode or review-to-work in plain mode, admission SHALL refuse at or above the configured limit. Moves outside the counted set SHALL retain existing transition guards and allow capacity release.
- Before classifying a requested mutation as counted or uncounted, admission SHALL require each counted status to resolve to exactly one normalized configured option label with a nonempty option ID. WHEN another configured label shares that counted ID, including another label within `statusFlow`, or counted option labels are ambiguous after normalization, admission SHALL refuse before mutation. This check SHALL enforce local counted-option consistency without claiming remote schema attestation.
- Admission SHALL reject malformed or incomplete snapshots, pagination hard-cap truncation, and missing or ambiguous target identity. The mutation SHALL use the actual item ID established by that snapshot and SHALL NOT substitute a cached/re-resolved identity without a new admission check. Cached occupancy SHALL NOT authorize a start.
- Cooperating direct and replay callers in one clone, including linked worktrees, SHALL serialize the fresh-read/check/mutation section through primary-checkout local state. Direct starts SHALL NOT acquire the queue flush lock; replay SHALL acquire the flush lock before the admission lock. The checkpoint SHALL be checked again after acquiring admission ownership.
- Admission SHALL release ownership on ordinary success, failure and handled signals after surviving foreground mutation children finish. A bounded lock-acquisition refusal SHALL make no mutation and SHALL report actionable busy or uncertain state. A potentially abandoned lock SHALL NOT be stolen based only on age; hard-crash recovery SHALL require verified-idle manual cleanup.
- Capacity and invalid-state refusals SHALL NOT newly queue a direct start. Proven GitHub rate-limit responses SHALL preserve existing deferred queue behavior. Queued starts SHALL recheck current capacity, remain recoverable when blocked or uninspectable, and permit unrelated queued operations under existing failure semantics. An already-target skip SHALL require a fresh complete snapshot and unambiguous target, with the checkpoint check preceding it.

## MODIFIED — §9.2 Enforcement limits

- The picker SHALL report selection-time headroom; the actual bundled mutation boundary SHALL independently enforce admission. Existing checkpoint, comment-steering, gate, red-first, review and closure guards SHALL remain necessary. Claude hooks SHALL remain defense in depth; `SERIAL_DELIVERY_OVERRIDE` SHALL override only its existing hook behavior, not command admission.
- Documentation SHALL state that this mutex is local serialization, not a slot ledger or distributed reservation service. Other clones, raw API writes and eventual consistency of GitHub reads SHALL remain explicit external limits; the implementation SHALL NOT claim an atomic GitHub count-and-mutate transaction or a globally enforced WIP ceiling.
