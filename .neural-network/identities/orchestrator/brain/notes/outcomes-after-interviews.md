Record brain-note outcomes AFTER the retro interviews, not before: the orchestrator marked a recalled dev note "useful" from its own read of the work, and the dev's interview then reported that note was correctly UNUSED that task (named functions meant the fallback technique never fired). An outcome recorded from the orchestrator's guess is exactly the false-signal the outcome system exists to avoid — the acting agent is the only source of truth for whether a recalled note actually fired.
