When an enforcement hook blocks a board transition because required records (review passes, validations) are missing from history, do the required activity retroactively against merged reality and record the genuine result — never fabricate the record and never leave the board lying. The byproduct is usually a crop of real follow-up findings; a retroactive two-pass review of two merged tasks surfaced eight. Sibling of [[dev-idle-recovery-ladder]].
