A brief must name which TODO numbers an agent owns, or agents tick each other's work.

## What happened
Two reviewers ran at once. One ticked the other's still-running passes, so the record claimed unfinished work.

## What to do
- List the agent's TODO numbers in the brief, and say which ones it must not touch.
- When a reviewer of one kind reports, check the record before moving on.
