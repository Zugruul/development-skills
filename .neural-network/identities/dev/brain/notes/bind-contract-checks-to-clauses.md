## Check the claim in its unique location
- A whole-section keyword match can accept opposite security or authority claims when another sentence supplies the expected words.
- Bind checks to the approved clause and its subject, provider relationship and negation. For a fixed document format, require unique ordered section boundaries before checking their content.
- Text present elsewhere, including a proposed-change appendix, does not prove a canonical clause was replaced. Reject duplicate marked tables and recognized headings instead of reading only the first match.
- Mutate ownership, authorization, isolation and disclosure statements into their opposites; require rejection in both pending and folded contracts.
- Keep this a narrow regression check, not a claim to understand arbitrary prose. Independent review still verifies the full document and legacy runtime branches.
