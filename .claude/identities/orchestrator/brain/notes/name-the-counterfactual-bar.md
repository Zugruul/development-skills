Review briefs that explicitly demand counterfactual verification — "strip the fix in an isolated copy and confirm the assertion flips", "verify the comment's claim against computed reality" — reliably get non-vacuous tests and catch comment-vs-behavior lies; reviewers rise to the bar the brief names. Generic "review the tests" briefs get reading, not evidence. Name the bar every time.
